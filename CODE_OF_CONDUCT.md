# Code of Conduct

This is a small community project. The rules are short.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient with each other.
- Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Stay on topic in issues, PRs, and Discussions.
- Use plain language and Australian or British English where it reads
  naturally.

## What is not OK

- Personal attacks, slurs, or targeted hostility toward any individual
  or group.
- Sustained derailing of conversations.
- Sharing other peoples private information without permission.
- Trying to pass off proprietary Line 6, Yamaha, or other third-party
  code, firmware, or assets as your own contribution.

## If something goes wrong

If you see behaviour that breaks the rules above, or you experience it
yourself, please contact the maintainer at
`dwaynecharrington@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled privately and in
good faith.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, lock threads, or block
accounts that repeatedly break these rules.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository, issues,
PRs, Discussions, and any other channel that represents the project.
