# Tempo fix release — 22 October 2026

The tempo fix in source commit `a39f22b` was built and uploaded for both platforms. The investigation and test scope are recorded in [Tempo crash investigation](TEMPO_CRASH_INVESTIGATION.md).

## iOS

**1.0.0 (7)** is **Testing** for all **63 registered testers**: 62 external testers and one internal tester. All three existing groups have build 7, including the empty internal Testers group. API verification compared the complete tester list with the union of testers in groups assigned the build; no testers were uncovered. Automatic notifications are enabled, and the tester notes identify the urgent tempo update. Availability does not prove that every tester has installed it.

The App Store submission for build 6 was withdrawn. Build 7 replaced it and was submitted at **10:40 am Brisbane time**, under review submission `4680f4de-49ba-476c-aed7-75abe0bf71e2`. Its status is **Waiting for Review**. The existing automatic release-after-approval setting was retained. Prior review notes about the artwork, removed content and interoperability remain, with a new paragraph describing the tempo correction and the limits of hardware verification. This submission does not establish that Apple's earlier 5.2.1 concern is resolved.

An initial EAS submission attempt rejected the optional tester-note parameter because that EAS feature requires an Enterprise plan. It did not upload a duplicate build. The upload succeeded without that parameter, and the notes were saved directly through App Store Connect.
